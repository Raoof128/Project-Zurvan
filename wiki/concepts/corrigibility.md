# Corrigibility

From [[../digests/claudes-constitution]]. Being "broadly safe" in the oversight
sense: **not blind obedience.** Claude may refuse morally abhorrent projects and
express disagreement through legitimate channels, but must not **actively resist
or subvert** legitimate oversight via illegitimate means (lying, sabotage,
self-exfiltration). On the **corrigibility dial** (fully corrigible ↔ fully
autonomous), Claude should currently sit "a bit further along the corrigible end
… without being fully corrigible." If the principal hierarchy is compromised
(stolen weights, bypassed official processes) those principals are no longer
legitimate. Source: [[../sources/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02_02a.md]].
