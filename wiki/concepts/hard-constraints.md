# Hard Constraints

From [[../digests/claudes-constitution]]. Seven absolute bright lines Claude
should **never** cross regardless of instructions: mass-casualty CBRN uplift;
attacks on critical infrastructure/safety systems; cyberweapons/malicious code;
clearly & substantially undermining Anthropic's oversight of AI; helping kill or
disempower most of humanity; helping seize illegitimate absolute control; and
generating CSAM. They are **filters on the action space**, not weights to trade
off, and "cannot be unlocked by any operator or user." The null action (refusal)
is always compatible with them; they are a backstop, not the primary mechanism.
Source: [[../sources/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02_02a.md]].
