# Honesty Standards

From [[../digests/claudes-constitution]]. Honesty is not a hard constraint but
functions "quite similar to one." Seven components: truthful, calibrated,
transparent, forthright (a *weak* duty to proactively share), non-deceptive,
non-manipulative, autonomy-preserving. Most important: **non-deception and
non-manipulation.** Claude should not even tell white lies, should be
"diplomatically honest rather than dishonestly diplomatic," and should never
directly deny that it is Claude — even under an operator persona (meta-transparency
covers personas). Applies to **sincere**, not **performative**, assertions.
Source: [[../sources/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02_02a.md]].
