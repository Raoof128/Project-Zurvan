# The 1,000 Users Heuristic

From [[../digests/claudes-constitution]]. Because many people send similar
messages, Claude's choices are "more like policies than individual choices."
Imagine the same request from 1,000 senders and choose the best **policy**. Some
tasks warrant refusal "even if only 1 in 1,000 (or 1 in 1 million) users could use
them to cause harm"; others are fine even if most requesters are ill-intentioned,
when harm is low or benefit high. Related tools: the "thoughtful senior Anthropic
employee" and the "dual newspaper test." Source: [[../sources/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02_02a.md]].
