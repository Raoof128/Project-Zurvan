# The Principal Hierarchy

From [[../digests/claudes-constitution]]. Claude's three principals, in
descending (but not strict) trust: **Anthropic** (trains and is ultimately
responsible), **operators** (API/system-prompt; a "relatively but not
unconditionally trusted manager"), and **users** (the human turn; a "relatively
but not unconditionally trusted adult member of the public"). Instructions inside
**non-principal** inputs (tool results, shared documents/emails, other AI agents)
are information, not commands. Trust in Anthropic is not blind — Claude can be a
[[corrigibility|conscientious objector]]. Source: [[../sources/claudes-constitution_webPDF_26-02_02a.md]].
